KEY DIFFERENCES:
================
1. Grid Layout:    4 albums → 2 albums per page
2. Album Flow:     Side-by-side → Stacked vertically  
3. Art Position:   Right side → Top
4. Track Position: Left side → Bottom
5. Track Names:    Truncated → Wrapped
6. Buttons:        70px → 55px (-21%)
7. Pages:          Fewer → More (but more space each)
